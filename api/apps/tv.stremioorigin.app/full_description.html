A native Stremio addon app, written in C.

You bring the account and addons; it ships empty.

  • Seek previews on the seek bar
  • Film collections, and the next film after the credits
  • Subtitles and audio remembered for each show
  • Subtitle fonts (Arabic too), colour, position and sync
  • Live TV, chapters, skip intro, spoiler blur
  • Dolby Vision, DTS sound, torrents with USB
  • 28 languages, Arabic included
